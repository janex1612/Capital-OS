The idea

Trading automation built around survival first.

Capital OS is not designed to chase the highest possible return. The goal is to build a disciplined, measurable system that prioritizes risk control, repeatability and long-term robustness across changing market conditions.

◫

Rule-based decisions

Entries require predefined market structure, trend, pullback/retest and confirmation conditions.

⬡

Risk before return

Position risk, portfolio exposure and drawdown limits are enforced before a trade can be opened.

↻

Test, measure, refine

Every rule must survive historical testing, simulated failures and extended demo validation before live use.

How it works

From market data to controlled execution.

The system processes market information through several independent checks. A trade only reaches execution if the setup and the account both pass the required conditions.

01 / DATA

Market input

Price, structure, volatility and trading-session context enter the system.

02 / SETUP

Signal detection

The engine looks for predefined trend, pullback, retest and confirmation patterns.

03 / QUALITY

Trade filtering

Weak, counter-trend, low-quality or unsuitable market conditions are rejected.

04 / RISK

Risk approval

Exposure, drawdown, trade risk and account safety checks must all pass.

05 / EXECUTION

Order & monitoring

Approved trades are executed, monitored and reconciled against broker state.

Safety architecture

The system is designed to fail safely.

Automated trading can fail for reasons unrelated to strategy: stale data, broker disconnects, duplicate orders, execution drift or software faults. Capital OS therefore treats operational safety as a core feature, not an add-on.

Capital protection

✓Hard position and portfolio risk limits
✓Weekly and overall drawdown protection
✓Exposure and position-count caps
✓Risk reduction before any new trade is allowed

Operational protection

✓Kill switch and safe-mode behavior
✓Broker and market-data health checks
✓Automatic state reconciliation
✓New trades blocked when system confidence is compromised
Validation roadmap

No meaningful capital before proof.

Capital OS follows a staged development path. Each phase must meet defined reliability and performance criteria before moving forward.

NOW

Core engineering

Finish execution, broker connectivity, monitoring and remaining system components.

NEXT

Historical backtesting

Test performance, drawdown behavior, expectancy and consistency across different market regimes.

THEN

Demo validation

Run the complete system in real market conditions without risking capital and compare results against the backtest assumptions.

LATER

Small live test

Only after passing prior stages, deploy a very small amount of real capital to validate execution under live conditions.

Success criteria

Robust performance

Results remain acceptable across different time periods and market conditions rather than one optimized sample.

Controlled drawdowns

Losses remain inside predefined limits and safety rules work exactly as intended under stress.

Live-test parity
